> **CityPulse Mumbai** is an evidence-backed, geospatial Decision Support System (DSS) designed for the **Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC)** and **Mumbai Police Infrastructure Planning Division**. It identifies locality-level safety vulnerability across **538 Mumbai observation units**, provides transparent mathematical factor decomposition of infrastructure deficits, and empowers municipal officers to explore hypothetical scenarios for patrol beats, auxiliary chowki deployments, and smart LED streetlighting upgrades.

## 🔬 Scientific Integrity & Analytical Methodology

### Closed-Form Index Reverse-Engineering
During our analytical audit, Ordinary Least Squares decomposition proved that the target `risk_index` is **100% deterministically generated by a closed-form linear formula** ($R^2 = 1.000$, residual $< 10^{-11}$):

$$\text{risk\_index} = 6.2726 + 0.1136(\text{crimes}) - 0.000072(\text{density}) + 0.4013(\text{police\_density}) + 0.9224(\text{uhi}) - 0.0870(\text{internet})$$

Rather than training an opaque machine learning model to predict this arithmetic tautology and falsely claiming "99.7% ML accuracy", CityPulse Mumbai adopts complete scientific transparency:
1. We document the exact formula weights as an **interpretable multi-criteria prioritization indicator**.
2. We evaluate its trade-offs against an independent **Naive Per-Capita Crime Baseline**.

## 🔍 Three Actionable Analytical Discoveries (EDA)

1. **The Chowki Deficit Strain:** Localities in the top population density quartile bear **$2.0\times$ higher incident load per police chowki** ($1.98\times$ locality mean ratio, $1.82\times$ pooled aggregate). Having 5 outposts is deceptive when resident density exceeds $50,000/\text{km}^2$.
2. **Built-Environment & UHI Risk Nexus:** High Urban Heat Island (UHI) zones record **$1.8\times$ higher recorded incidents against women** (12.2 vs 6.7), with a statistically significant continuous Spearman rank correlation of $\mathbf{\rho = 0.3623}$ ($p = 3.95 \times 10^{-18}$). UHI serves as an empirical proxy for unlit, high-density, narrow built corridors, giving municipal engineers direct quantitative justification to bundle public lighting and urban greening with public safety.
3. **Digital Reporting Inequity:** Localities in the lowest digital connectivity quartile exhibit a **$+5.9$ point higher risk score** (14.03 vs 8.16, Spearman $\rho = -0.3117$), despite reporting fewer digital complaints. Relying purely on online complaints creates institutional bias against digitally underserved communities.

## 📜 Ethical Safeguards & Disclaimers

- **Planning Prototype Status:** CityPulse Mumbai is an analytical decision-support prototype for municipal resource prioritization and does NOT replace real-time emergency dispatch (Call Police: 100/112 | Women Helpline: 1091).
- **Hypothetical Simulator Framing:** The scenario explorer tests heuristic planning trade-offs and does not guarantee real-world crime reduction.
- **Reporting Bias Disclaimer:** Crime statistics reflect recorded FIRs and may under-represent incidents in areas with high reporting friction. CityPulse explicitly balances raw crime counts with structural indicators.
- **Demonstration Data Provenance:** The 538 records are compilation/demonstration units mixing street corridors, neighborhoods, and administrative wards. Official agencies in India report at ward and police-station levels, not street level.
- **Scope Exclusion:** Payment safety and cyber fraud data are strictly excluded from physical urban street safety prioritization.
